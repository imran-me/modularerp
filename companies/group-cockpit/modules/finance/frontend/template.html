How consolidation works

why the group column differs from the sum of concerns

When one concern sells to another, the seller books 1300 Inter-company Receivable and the buyer books 2400 Inter-company Payable for the same amount. Across the group these are the same money owed to itself, so on consolidation they are moved into the Elimination column and their Group figure nets to zero — the group only reports what it is owed by, and owes to, outside parties.
